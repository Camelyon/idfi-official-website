ABOUT & TOKENS

IDFI is a liquidity-first DeFi ecosystem built around soft-pegged assets and transparent on-chain liquidity infrastructure. IDOLL is the first active ecosystem asset, while IDEU and the future IDFI governance and utility token remain part of the long-term roadmap.

IDOLL

Independent Dollar

IDOLL is a USD-referenced soft-pegged digital asset currently operating on BNB Smart Chain. Its primary market is the IDOLL/USDT PancakeSwap V3 liquidity pool. Public market activity, liquidity and token information can be independently verified through the official IDOLL website and supported blockchain analytics platforms.
Official IDOLL Website Live Market View on BscScan

IDEU

Independent Euro

IDEU is a Euro-referenced soft-pegged asset that gives DeFi users simple EUR exposure without complex collateral or borrowing. It is designed to sit in a target price range around the Euro while staying highly liquid in AMM pools. Together with IDOLL, IDEU forms the core FX pair of the IDFI ecosystem.

IDFI

Independent Digital Finance Token

IDFI is the governance and revenue-sharing token of the ecosystem. It captures fees and LP rewards generated by IDFI liquidity pools and automated market-making strategies. Over time, active contributors, LP providers and strategic partners will be able to earn IDFI based on transparent, on-chain metrics.

LIQUIDITY PROVIDERS

Independent liquidity providers help deepen the IDOLL / USDT market, improve swap execution and strengthen the long-term resilience of the IDFI on-chain ecosystem.

◈

Transparent On-Chain Participation

Liquidity is supplied directly to the public PancakeSwap V3 pool. Pool balances, positions, swaps and market activity remain independently verifiable through blockchain explorers and public analytics platforms.

↔

Trading-Fee Participation

Active LP positions may earn a share of the trading fees generated inside their selected PancakeSwap V3 price ranges. Fee income is determined by actual pool activity, position range and deployed liquidity.

↗

Liquidity-First Market Growth

IDFI supports continuous and measurable on-chain market activity. This can help active LP positions encounter recurring fee-generating swaps, but returns are never fixed, guaranteed or risk-free.

Liquidity provider notice: Providing liquidity involves price-range, impermanent-loss, smart-contract, token-price and market risks. Fee income varies with trading activity and position management. Participation does not constitute a deposit, interest product or guaranteed passive return.
